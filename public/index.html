About LogLens

LogLens eliminates the 3 a.m. log-scrolling nightmare by turning massive raw log files into a handful of ranked, explained incidents in under a second.

  • 100% Local & Offline — runs completely on the server with zero external API keys.
  • Rule-Free Pattern Mining — placeholder normalisation and Drain template mining (plus a TF-IDF fallback in local installs) group unseen errors automatically.
  • Transparent Impact Ranking — every group is scored 0–100 across five explainable factors with live-tunable weights and a likely-trigger cascade detection.
